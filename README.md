This project is a group work made by Loic Loblot and Loann Ravel for the university unit Techniques for empirical reaserch. 
It is a little game with 5 levels with a difficulty increased every level passed. 
You will be a little brain and you will have to kill/escape from little viruses by jumping and moving from left to right to achieve a level. 
Hoping you will enjoy it.
